<h2>Project description</h2>

The Municipal Fleet Management Portal is a web application developed to help manage municipal vehicles and their related records. The system allows authorized users to view and update vehicle information, record fuel transactions, track maintenance, and manage vehicle key assignments based on their assigned permissions.
